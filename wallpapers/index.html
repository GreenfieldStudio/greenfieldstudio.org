Coral Reef

A living coral reef with its own fish behaviour, currents and sunlight. Clownfish nestle in their anemones, blue tangs and moorish idols drift through the kelp, a lionfish patrols the rocks, a manta ray glides overhead and a school of silver fish wheels past.

It is the same world as the Coral Reef films on the Ambience page.

Fish models made with the help of generative AI.

Watch the reef films first

world 2 · after the reef · no date yet

Enchanted Castle

A castle in an enchanted forest, followed through a whole day, from before dawn into a magical night. A river runs past to the waterfall, the bell rings on the hour, and now and then a dragon flies over.

It is the world of the Enchanted Castle film.
