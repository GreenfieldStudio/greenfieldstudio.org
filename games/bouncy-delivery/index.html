Bouncy Delivery

Drag, aim and bounce parcels into your neighbours' windows. Missed throws turn into ramps, so every miss helps your next delivery.

Aim, throw, bounce

fig. 02 how to play

Three moves

  1. Dragfrom the parcel toward the glowing window. A dotted path shows the throw.
  2. Releaseto throw. Use a mouse or your finger.
  3. BounceA missed parcel stays where it lands as a ramp. Bounce the next one off it.

R restarts the stop and Esc pauses. The speaker button mutes the sound. On a phone held upright, an aim pad sits under the picture.

On a phone, upright

Hold the phone upright and the street shrinks to the top of the screen. Below it, a large aim pad shows your drag and the next parcel. The game also plays on its side and on a desktop.

fig. 03 what is in it

36 stops, six neighbourhoods

Misses become ramps

Wherever a parcel lands, it becomes a ramp you can bounce the next parcel off.

New twists as you go

Awnings and balconies get in the way. Springy ramps launch you higher, and one-way ramps only bounce parcels moving to the right.

Three stars per stop

Use few parcels, stick to one ramp, or deliver without a single miss. Stars unlock six parcel styles.

The stops run from Sunshine Street to Festival Heights. Sound is simple beeps and chimes, with no music and no voice. The game is in English, and your progress is saved on your device.
