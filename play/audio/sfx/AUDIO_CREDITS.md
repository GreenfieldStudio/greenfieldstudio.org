## Sound effects

**`door.ogg`** — derived from *"door-open.wav"* by **Qat**
([freesound.org/s/107640](https://freesound.org/people/Qat/sounds/107640/)), licensed
**CC0 1.0**. Qat's sound is itself a modification of *"hugeSlidingDoorSlamECM800.wav"* by
**UATaudio** ([freesound.org/s/31154](https://freesound.org/people/UATaudio/sounds/31154/)),
licensed **CC BY 3.0** — https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/3.0/. Both are credited here in
respect of that upstream licence. Modified (trimmed, re-encoded to OGG Vorbis) for use in this
game.

**`holeinone.ogg`** — *"Achievment Chimes.wav"* by **LaurenPonder**
([freesound.org/s/635665](https://freesound.org/people/LaurenPonder/sounds/635665/)), licensed
**CC0 1.0**. Credited voluntarily; CC0 requires no attribution. Modified (trimmed, re-encoded to
OGG Vorbis).

**`boost.ogg`** — by **Iwan "qubodup" Gabovitch**
([freesound.org/people/qubodup](https://freesound.org/people/qubodup/)). Credited per the
author's stated attribution preference. Modified (trimmed, re-encoded to OGG Vorbis).

**`break.ogg`** — by **Tobiasz "unfa" Karoń**
([freesound.org/people/unfa](https://freesound.org/people/unfa/)). Modified (trimmed, re-encoded
to OGG Vorbis).

Remaining sound effects in this directory carry no authorship metadata and are pending source
confirmation. Many sounds in the game are generated procedurally at runtime and involve no
third-party material.

## Music

All music in this game is original first-party work by **GreenfieldStudio** — the per-world stems
were composed in [BandLab](https://www.bandlab.com/) or synthesized programmatically for this
project. No third-party music is used.

If you are a rightsholder and believe your work is used here without correct credit or licence,
please get in touch and it will be corrected or removed promptly.
