Privacy Policy

Minigolf Pro — Greenfield Studio · Last updated: September 2026

Short version: this version of Minigolf Pro does not collect or transmit any personal data. There is no account, no sign-in and no cloud save. Your progress is stored only in your own browser, on your own device.

Who we are

Minigolf Pro is developed by Greenfield Studio. This policy covers the version of the game published on web game portals such as Poki.

Data we collect

None. This build of the game has no account system, no sign-in, and no connection to any database or analytics service operated by us. Specifically, we do not collect:

  • email addresses, names, or any other account details — the game never asks for them;
  • gameplay or usage analytics;
  • crash or error reports;
  • location, contacts, device identifiers, or advertising identifiers.

Where your progress is stored

Your scores, unlocked items and settings are written to your browser's local storage on your own device. They are never uploaded to us. Because the data lives in your browser, clearing your browser data — or playing in a private/incognito window — will remove your progress, and it cannot be recovered by us, since we never had a copy.

Advertising and the game portal

The portal hosting this game displays advertisements and uses its own software development kit to do so. That advertising, and any data collected in connection with it, is handled by the portal and its advertising partners under their privacy policies, not this one. If you are playing on Poki, see Poki's privacy policy. We do not receive, store or have access to any data gathered by the portal or its advertising partners, and we do not sell data.

Children

The game is suitable for a general audience and is not directed at collecting information from children — because it does not collect information from anyone. Where the game is played on a portal, that portal's own protections and policies for younger players apply.

Your rights

Data protection law gives you rights to access, correct and delete personal data held about you. As we hold no personal data from this version of the game, there is nothing for us to retrieve or erase. You can remove your locally stored progress at any time by clearing your browser's site data. For any data collected by the portal, please contact the portal directly.

Other versions of this game

Minigolf Pro is also distributed on other platforms, where optional accounts and cloud saves are available. Those versions collect more than this one, and carry their own policy. This page describes the version you are playing right now.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the "last updated" date above will change with it. Material changes will be reflected in the game's own in-app policy as well.
