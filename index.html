fig. 05 the studio

Every hole gets played thousands of times before you do.

A physics simulator checks each course for fairness, possible routes, and how a first-time player might fare. Then it is tuned by hand until it is simply fun to play.

Greenfield Studio is an independent, one-person studio. We build games and worlds the way a surveyor maps a field: slowly, and by measuring everything.

The same patience goes into Greenfield Ambience: real-time 3D worlds, recorded one frame at a time in 4K, where nothing loops.
