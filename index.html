fig. 02 the studio

Small games, patiently measured.

Greenfield Studio is an independent, one-person game studio. We build games the way a surveyor maps a field: slowly, and by measuring everything.

Every hole in Minigolf Pro is played thousands of times by a physics simulator before anyone else sees it. Each one is checked for fairness, for how many routes it allows, and for how a first-time player really gets on. Then it's tuned by hand until it is simply fun to play.

  • 01 · instant

    Play in a second. Everything runs in a browser tab. No download, no install, no account.

  • 02 · fair

    Physics you can read. When a shot misses, you can see why and line up the next one.

  • 03 · for everyone

    Made to be played by anyone. Colorblind palettes, reduced motion, touch or mouse, and six languages.
