01 descriptions

Short

Free physics minigolf — 80 holes across 8 worlds, a full level editor, and online multiplayer. Plays right in your browser.

Long

Minigolf Pro is a complete 2D minigolf game that plays instantly in any modern browser, on desktop or phone, with no download and no paywall. Players thread trick shots through 80 holes across eight distinct worlds, from a gentle Meadow to slick Arctic ice, built from spinners, cannons, portals, magnets, moving platforms, breakable walls, slopes and wind.

A full level editor with 20+ tools lets anyone build, validate and publish their own holes, then share them as links, playlists and gauntlets. Online rooms seat two to four players, with chat and ghost replays. Daily and weekly challenges, a community Hole of the Week, leaderboards, a human-feeling CPU with eight personalities, and deep ball customization round it out.

Key features

  • 80 holes across 8 themed worlds, including 8 mastery finales
  • Readable, deterministic physics: every hole is simulated thousands of times before it ships
  • Full level editor with community sharing, playlists and gauntlets
  • Online multiplayer for 2–4 players, plus local play and CPU opponents
  • Random 9, Endless, Drill and Hard Mode; daily and weekly challenges
  • Colorblind, high-contrast, reduced-motion and left-handed options; six languages

About Greenfield Studio

Greenfield Studio is an independent, one-person game studio making small, carefully measured physics games that play instantly in the browser. Minigolf Pro is its first game. greenfieldstudio.org
