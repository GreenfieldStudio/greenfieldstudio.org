Licensing & custom renders

Greenfield Ambience films are calm, real-time 3D worlds with no music. If you run a café, spa, clinic, hotel or co-working space, you can ask for a licence to show them on your screens, or for a custom render of a world.

There is no checkout on this page. You write to us, and we answer by email with a quote and the terms in writing. Contact us for a quote.

Business display licence

For places that want something calm on the wall. The licence lets you show the films on screens at your business, for your customers and your staff.

  • There is no music in the films, so there are no music rights to clear.
  • A licence covers one location. Several locations need one licence each.
  • The films are not for resale, and not for uploading or sharing outside your business.
  • This page is not a binding offer. The quote and the terms we send you in writing, before you pay anything, are what count.

The world you can ask for

The Cabin. A wooden cabin at night. In the two Halloween films below, rain patters on the roof and runs down the windows, thunder rolls over the mountains, and a fire crackles in the hearth. The sound is generated by our own software, with no recordings and no music.

Those two films are set on Halloween night. Watch one before you write, and tell us if it suits your space:

Cozy Halloween Cabin Ambience (3 hours)
Cozy Halloween Cabin for Sleep (8 hours)

The other worlds on the Ambience page are not part of this offer yet.

Custom render

Choose the weather, the time of day and how long it runs, and we render the Cabin that way on our own computer and send you the video. What we can change depends on the world, so ask first and we will tell you what is possible. The two Halloween cabin films are night scenes, and when we can deliver depends on our render schedule. Contact us for a quote.

How to ask

Write to greenfieldstudiodev@gmail.com. The buttons below open an email with the subject and a short form already filled in.

Ask about a display licence Ask about a custom render

Tell us:

  • What kind of business you run, and in which town and country.
  • How many locations you want to cover.
  • Which world you want. Today that is the Cabin.
  • For a custom render: the weather, the time of day and the length you have in mind.
