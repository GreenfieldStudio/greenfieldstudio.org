Play, build, play together

Pick a course, make one of your own, or meet friends online.

Physics & hazards Level editor Online rooms

Real physics, readable and fair

Spinners, cannons, moving platforms, magnets, portals, breakable walls, slopes, wind and more. Every mechanic behaves the same way every time, so a missed shot always teaches you something.

Before a hole ships it has been played thousands of times by a physics simulator, and tuned until it's fair for first-timers and still rewarding for experts.

Build anything: a full level editor

A creation suite with 20+ tools. Place any mechanic, drag-tune the layout, and the editor checks that your hole is actually solvable before you publish it.

Share the link, then browse and play a growing library of community holes: like, sort and follow your favorite creators. Bundle favorites into playlists and gauntlets and share those too.

Inside the level editor

Play together, online Available now

  • 2–4 player rooms, public or invite-only, matched or by code
  • Any of the 8 worlds together, in classic, Hard or Mirror variants
  • In-room chat and ghost replays of every shot

Coming later Ranked 1v1 (a Bronze to Diamond ladder) and tournaments are built and on the way. They switch on once the server-side match validator lands.

More ways to play

  • Random 9: a fresh 9-hole mix every run
  • Endless: procedurally generated holes that never run out
  • Drill: focused practice on a single mechanic, or a chain of them
  • Hard Mode: every hole mirrored and obstacle-hardened, the campaign at maximum challenge

Plus a Daily Challenge with a streak to keep, a Weekly Challenge, a community-picked Hole of the Week, per-hole and weekly leaderboards, and GIF replays of your best shots to save and share.

Make it yours

Earn coins just by playing and unlock dozens of ball skins, trails, celebrations and auras, plus a face builder (expressions, animals and legendary lookalikes) and outfits. 100+ achievements to chase.

Opponents that feel human

The CPU is sim-trained to play like a person. It lays up, runs putts past, yips the occasional gimme, and comes in 8 named personalities across three difficulty tiers.

Built for everyone

Colorblind palettes, high-contrast and reduced-motion modes. Full touch controls in portrait or landscape, with a left-handed mode.

Six languages

English, Deutsch, 中文, Português (BR), Français and Español.

Three steps, then a lifetime of lining it up

  1. Drag backfrom the ball to aim. The further you pull, the harder the shot.
  2. Releaseto putt. On a phone, touch and drag does the same.
  3. Sink itin as few strokes as you can, then chase the ace.

100% free. Plays in any modern browser.
